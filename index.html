About Mighty Banyan

Support grounded in compassion, dignity, and connection.

Like the banyan tree, we can grow stronger through connection, support, and deeply rooted resilience.

Reaching out for counselling can feel difficult. At Mighty Banyan Counselling, you will be welcomed into a supportive and non-judgmental space where your experiences, concerns, and goals are treated with care and respect.

Counselling is collaborative. Together, we can explore the challenges you are facing, recognize your strengths, and develop practical ways to create meaningful and sustainable change.

Services are tailored to each client's circumstances, values, culture, and preferred pace.

Fees and payment

Professional counselling at an accessible rate.

Individual counselling

$100

per 50-minute session

The fee reflects a conscious commitment to accessibility. Online delivery and a low-overhead practice model make it possible to offer experienced, professional counselling at a rate that is more manageable for many clients.

Mighty Banyan Counselling is committed to maintaining a high standard of professional care. Information about the practitioner’s credentials, experience, training, and relevant certifications is available on this website.

Appointments are arranged directly by email or phone. Payment of $100 by e-transfer must be received at least 24 hours before the scheduled session. Once payment is received, the appointment is confirmed and the client will receive instructions for joining the secure online session through Jane.

Payments are non-refundable. Clients may reschedule an appointment by providing at least 24 hours’ notice. Appointments cancelled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours’ notice will result in the session fee being forfeited.

If the provider is unable to keep the appointment, a replacement appointment will be arranged at no additional cost.

Direct billing is not available. A receipt will be provided and may be submitted to an insurance provider for reimbursement, depending on the terms of the client’s plan.
