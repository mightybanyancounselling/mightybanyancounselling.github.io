Meet Rupa

Support grounded in compassion, dignity, and connection.

Like the banyan tree, we can grow stronger through connection, support, and deeply rooted resilience.

Hello, I’m Rupa Ray, MSW, RSW, a Registered Social Worker in Alberta and Ontario. I created Mighty Banyan Counselling to offer a warm, supportive, and non-judgmental space where people can feel heard, respected, and understood.

My approach is trauma-informed, culturally responsive, and collaborative. Counselling is tailored to each person’s experiences, values, culture, circumstances, and preferred pace rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach.

I also believe that high-quality counselling should be accessible. Mighty Banyan was created with the goal of providing thoughtful, evidence-informed therapy while keeping care within reach for more individuals and families.

Together, we can explore the challenges you are facing, recognize your strengths, better understand patterns that may be affecting you, and develop practical ways to create meaningful and sustainable change.

Counselling is available in English, Bengali, Hindi, and Urdu.

Why Mighty Banyan?

Broad experience. Deeply connected roots.

Like a banyan tree, strength can come from growing many roots while remaining connected to one foundation.

A banyan tree grows differently from most trees. As its branches extend outward, they send down new roots—creating a wider, stronger foundation while remaining part of the same tree.

That image reflects how I think about clinical experience. Working with people across healthcare, trauma services, rehabilitation, newcomer communities, chronic illness, workplace recovery, relationships, and private practice has not meant moving from one disconnected area to another. Each setting has added another perspective and another root to the same clinical foundation.

People rarely arrive in counselling with just one issue. Mental health can be connected with relationships, physical health, work, family, culture, migration, trauma, grief, and major life changes. A broad clinical background helps me understand these connections and work with the whole person rather than only a diagnosis or presenting concern.

Learn about these approaches
Trauma-focused

EMDR & Accelerated Resolution Therapy

Rupa uses trauma-focused approaches including EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART). She has completed both basic and advanced ART training and actively incorporates trauma-focused work into her clinical practice.

Structured & practical

CBT & CBT-I

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) can help explore the connections between thoughts, emotions, behaviours, and everyday patterns. Rupa also has training in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), allowing sleep concerns to be addressed more specifically when appropriate.

Acceptance & regulation

ACT & DBT

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT) offer tools for working with difficult emotions, developing psychological flexibility, strengthening coping skills, and moving toward actions that reflect what matters to you.

Meaning & perspective

Narrative & Strengths-Based Therapy

Narrative and strengths-based approaches make room for the broader story of a person’s life rather than defining someone by a problem or diagnosis. They can help identify resilience, values, abilities, relationships, and alternative ways of understanding difficult experiences.

Change & problem solving

Motivational Interviewing & Solution-Focused Therapy

These collaborative approaches can be useful when a client is considering change, feeling uncertain about next steps, or looking for practical ways to build on what is already working.

Relationships & context

Attachment, Family & Relational Approaches

Relationship patterns, attachment experiences, family dynamics, culture, and important life relationships can all shape emotional well-being. Rupa can draw on attachment-based, family, and relational perspectives when these connections are relevant to the work.

Fees & payment

Experienced professional counselling at an intentionally accessible rate.

Accessible pricing is a deliberate part of the Mighty Banyan model. It does not mean a reduced standard of care.

Over the years, Rupa has seen clients step away from counselling sooner than they would have liked because their extended health benefits were exhausted after only a small number of sessions. For many people, four or five sessions may provide a helpful beginning, but may not offer enough time to work through more complex or longstanding concerns.

Mighty Banyan’s fees are therefore set intentionally. The goal is to make high-quality, experienced counselling more accessible and to help clients stretch their available benefits further, so that cost is less likely to interrupt therapy before the work feels complete.

Appointments are arranged directly. Payment by e-transfer must be received at least 24 hours before the scheduled session. Once payment is received, the appointment is confirmed and instructions for joining the secure online session are provided.

Payments are non-refundable. Clients may reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice. Appointments cancelled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours’ notice will result in the session fee being forfeited.

If the provider is unable to keep the appointment, a replacement appointment will be arranged at no additional cost.

Direct billing is not available. A receipt will be provided. Coverage for counselling provided by a Registered Social Worker varies by insurance plan, so clients are encouraged to confirm their own benefits before booking.

Practice information

A few practical details before you begin.

Where are sessions offered?

Mighty Banyan Counselling is an online practice. Rupa is registered in Alberta and Ontario, and services are provided within the jurisdictions in which she is professionally registered.

Will my insurance cover the session?

Many extended health plans include counselling provided by a Registered Social Worker, but coverage varies. Mighty Banyan does not direct bill. You will receive a receipt that you can submit to your insurer where eligible.

What should I expect when I first get in touch?

You can email Mighty Banyan with a brief description of what you are looking for and your general availability. Rupa can then determine whether the service appears to be an appropriate fit and discuss next steps for booking.

What about confidentiality?

Privacy and confidentiality are central to counselling. The limits of confidentiality, record keeping, informed consent, and online-session practices are reviewed as part of beginning services.

Is Mighty Banyan an emergency or crisis service?

No. Mighty Banyan does not provide emergency or crisis-response services. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service in your area.

Ready to see whether Mighty Banyan feels like the right fit?

You are welcome to get in touch with a question or to request an appointment. A brief email about what you are looking for and your general availability is a good place to start.
